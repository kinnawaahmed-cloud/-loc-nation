### WHO WE ARE
We are 200 families of the indigenous fishing community of Kazinga Channel, Katwe-Kabatooro Town Council, Kasese District, Queen Elizabeth National Park, Uganda.

Our grandfathers settled here in 1942, before the park. We are not visitors - we are natives of Kazinga.

### OUR HISTORY & LEGEND
- **1942**: First fishing families settled at Kazinga Channel
- **1952**: Queen Elizabeth National Park created around our existing village
- **1993 - THE LION FIGHT**: One of our brave fishermen fought a lion with bare hands to protect his family and boats at Kazinga Channel. He survived! This is our bravery - Loc people fear nothing! This story is told to every child.
- **Today**: 200 families still fishing, still protecting our heritage.

### OUR FISH - TYPES WE CATCH IN KAZINGA CHANNEL
We are professional fishermen of Kazinga. We catch:
- **Tilapia** (Engege) - Our main fish
- **Mudfish** (Emale)
- **Lungfish** (Emmamba)
- **Silverfish** (Mukene / Muziri)
- **Catfish**
- **Moo Fish**

All fresh from Kazinga Channel every morning! From water to plate in 1 hour!

### OUR HERITAGE
- Traditional wooden boats & handmade nets
- Fishing knowledge passed from grandfather to grandson since 1942
- Living daily with elephants, hippos, buffalo, lions
- Our own community laws and leadership
- Fresh fish smoking and drying culture
