## WE ARE THE PROTECTORS

**To show the world through media, letting them know the protectors of wildlife and nature - we must protect what you have.**

We are the original protectors of Kazinga Channel. Before Queen Elizabeth National Park, our fishing village lived WITH the hippos, elephants, and birds - not against them.

### OUR MESSAGE TO THE WORLD:

> "We do not own nature, we PROTECT it. Our fathers fished Kazinga Channel since 1942 and kept the wildlife safe. True conservation comes from the people who live with nature every day."

### WHAT WE PROTECT:
- 🦛 Hippos of Kazinga Channel
- 🐘 Elephants of Queen Elizabeth
- 🐦 600+ Bird species
- 🐊 Wildlife and fishing balance
- 🌊 Kazinga Channel water itself

### OUR HERITAGE:
- **1942:** Kazinga Fishing Village formed - living with wildlife
- **1952:** Park formed around US - we stayed as protectors
- **2026:** Loc Nation formed to show world our conservation story

### CALL TO ACTION:
Through media, photos, and videos, we will show the world that local fishermen are the best protectors of nature. Protect what you have!
