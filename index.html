Our Story - Darkness Is Not Negative

Our grandparents started fishing here on Lake Edward. They protected the animals and birds. They lived together with them because they knew this heritage would be important for our grandchildren.

Today, Kazinga Channel is one of the best tourist attractions in Uganda. Tourism companies come every day, but our community gets nothing.

We are 400 youth who completed O-Level and A-Level. Our parents paid all school fees. Now there are no jobs, only fishing.

We have the land. We have the heritage. We have the youth. We are building a community-owned tourism site. Not charity. Partnership.
